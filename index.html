Cockpit — Every Control

  • Twin gauge pods — speedo left, tach right, and the Driver Information Center between them: trip, tire pressures, oil life, and battery voltage (his photo reads 13.9 V).
  • Wheel controls — cruise on the left spoke, DIC menu and audio on the right. The wheel turns itself in Lucian mode; you work the dash.
  • MyLink 7″ screen — Radio, phone, settings and the backup camera, on the car's actual screen in the center stack.
  • Radio / HVAC stack — volume and tune knobs, fan and temperature dials, A/C and recirc under the screen.
  • 4-pack aux gauges — oil pressure, oil temperature, battery volts and transmission temp, low on the console ahead of the shifter.
  • Shifter & console — gear select, sport mode, traction control and the hazard switch.

Reading The Car

  • DIC first — the center display is the car's own handbook page: it reports tire pressure per corner, oil-life percentage and voltage before anything feels wrong.
  • Aux gauges are the truth — watch oil pressure and trans temp on a hard DMV run; the 4-pack is there so the V6 can be driven hard and honestly.
  • Door-jamb sticker wins — tire pressures and load limits live on the driver's door jamb, not on the internet. Check there first.
  • 86,885 and counting — every number in this handbook traces to the real car, not a brochure car.
